# Statue scan catalog — threedscans.com

Era-appropriate sculpture scans for the low-poly statue pipeline
(`scripts/make-low-poly-statue.py`; full recipe in
[../reference/art-pipelines.md](../reference/art-pipelines.md)). All from
threedscans.com (Oliver Laric — published without restrictions), download
links verified live August 2026 (134 catalog entries crawled; 132 live —
"Jüngling vom Magdalensberg" and "Right Foot of Fanny Elssler" are dead).
Sizes are the compressed download. Baseline: 600 tris, flat-shaded.

Renaissance patrons collected antiquities and commissioned all'antica works,
so classical subjects are era-correct almost by definition. A few entries are
18th–19th-century neoclassical pieces in classical style — visually
indistinguishable at 600 tris; flagged *(neocl.)*.

## Known gaps

- **No full equestrian statue** — "The Bronze Horseman" and "Equestrian
  Monument of the Condottiere" have no scan match; the Medici Riccardi horse
  head is the closest gesture. Procedural stays the fallback.
- **No Pietà / Madonna group** for the two Pietà titles.
- Excluded whole categories: photosculptures, 19th-c. portrait statues
  (Napoleon, Beethoven, Einstein, Tennyson, Wesley, actors), natural-history
  scans (crabs, shells, sea stars, bones), Cambodian plaster casts, armor.

## Pipeline notes

- Full catalog with URLs + liveness: crawl via WP REST (`wp-json/wp/v2/posts`)
  then scrape each entry page for the `stlDL` href — the REST content field
  does not include download links.
- Scans arrive z-up, sometimes upside down, with turntable floaters; the
  script auto-handles all three. OBJ zips can carry a `__MACOSX` sidecar.
- Some scans keep their own socle/base — currently kept (reads as intentional
  base-on-plinth).
