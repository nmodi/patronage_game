## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** One of the most authored game HUDs at this scale — "The Patron's Ledger" is real on screen: parchment double-hairline panels, small-caps Goudy running heads, sienna-only command color, cloth pennants, in-fiction naming, night-sky menu. No generic city builder could wear it unchanged. Genericism leaks in exactly two places: the build flyout (rows of gold Lucide icons on plain cards) and the gallery, where every one of 12 artworks is the identical placeholder glyph — fatal to "art is the point." Specificity is carried by chrome and copy; the artifacts the game is *about* are its least specific pixels.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI: 3 advisory `design-system-color` findings, all likely false positives (low-alpha neutral scrims/shadows: FactionBanner.tsx:60 gradient shading over a palette color, nightSky.tsx:33 title text-shadow — both sanctioned by DESIGN.md). In-page detector (headless Firefox, demo city): 19 findings — `undersized-ui-text` ×9 (10px top-bar resource labels + materials-rail rate lines, below the 11px floor), `dark-glow` ×7 (all #28190a — the sanctioned panel-float shadow, likely misclassified against the dark loading backdrop), `layout-transition` ×2 (`transition: width` on the unfurl grammar — animates layout, not transform), `low-contrast` ×2 (4.2:1 ink-faint #7d6b4f on parchment-deep #ede7da, need 4.5:1). The detector independently confirmed the review's contrast concern with an exact ratio.

**Visual overlays:** browser run was headless — no human-visible overlay tab exists; console evidence only (fallback signal reported).

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The assign decision is data-starved and shouts four times.** Four stacked identical sienna "Assign to Bottega di …" primaries with no rank/XP/status shown, plus possible duplicate bottega names. This is the game's central decision and it breaks the One Voice rule while forcing panel-hopping. Fix: one ledger-line row per eligible bottega (name left, "Apprentice Painter · idle" right) with a single quiet Assign each; disambiguate the name pool. → /impeccable shape
- **[P1] Completion has no moment.** No notification when a commission finishes — the reward loop of the entire product ends in silence. Fix: reuse the OfferAlert grammar — "A work is finished" card: title, requester, favor +8, quality, [View in Gallery]. → /impeccable delight
- **[P2] Unexplained disabled states in the build palette.** Palazzo/Cathedral dim at opacity-50 with no reason (florins vs stone?). Players can't learn the materials system from the surface that gates on it. Fix: tint the short line item sienna, or hover line "Needs 34 more stone." → /impeccable clarify
- **[P2] Hover-only knowledge + accessibility floor.** Prestige checklist, population limiter, building tooltips, materials-rail detail all mouse-gated; Gallery ignores Esc; detector confirms 4.2:1 ink-faint-on-parchment-deep text and nine 10px functional labels below the 11px floor; commissions badge outside the crest button's accessible name. Fix: focusable ResourceStat with focus-within reveal, Esc on the modal, nudge ink-faint darker on parchment-deep, 11px labels. → /impeccable audit
- **[P3] Gold discipline + doc drift + layout-animating transitions.** Palette building icons in prestige-gold (committed to tallies only); materials rail left-docked while DESIGN.md says right; unfurl animates `width` (layout) rather than transform. → /impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (power user):** no hotkeys for Build categories or panels; Space/1–3/R/Shift-snap undocumented in-product; Gallery won't Esc-close; speed caps at 3× with no fast-forward.
- **Sam (accessibility):** favor-tier pip is color-alone; offer-count badge outside the accessible name (SR users never hear "1 offer"); 4.2:1 labels below AA; all tooltip content mouse-gated.
- **Piera (cozy Dorfromantik player):** permanently sienna House Medici pennant reads as a standing alert; expiring offers quietly cost favor (countdown only under 4 months); gallery of a dozen works looks like a file manager (identical thumbnails); no celebration for a finished artwork — the exact beat she came for.

## Questions to Consider

- What if a commission were a *document* — a folded letter with the faction's seal, opened on the ledger — instead of a list row?
- The gallery is the trophy room of a game about art and every trophy is the same icon. Cheapest path to unique per-work thumbnails (title-seeded procedural compositions)?
- If offer alert, commissions panel, and completion event were one continuous ledger surface, would the HUD need three commission touchpoints?
